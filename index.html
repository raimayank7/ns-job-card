Free job card and service bill maker

Write down what came in, what the customer asked for and the estimate, hand over a signed job card, track every job to delivery, then turn the same job into a service bill with parts and labour. For garages, mobile and computer repair, and appliance service.

How to make a job card here

  1. Type your shop once. Name, address, phone, GSTIN if registered and UPI ID. They print on every job card and bill.
  2. Book the item in. Customer and phone, then the item: type, make, model, registration or serial or IMEI, odometer for vehicles, what came with it and its condition. Write the complaint in the customer’s words.
  3. Give an estimate and a date. Type the estimate, the promised date and the technician. Print the job card in two copies and get the customer’s signature on the terms.
  4. Track the job. Change the status as work moves: in progress, waiting for parts, ready. "Open jobs" lists every job not yet delivered, oldest promise first.
  5. Add parts and labour. Each line is a part (at its own GST rate) or labour (repair service, SAC 9987). A warning shows when the bill goes more than 10% over the estimate.
  6. Bill and deliver. Set the status to Delivered: the record prints as a service bill. Mark it paid, or leave the balance due; send the customer a WhatsApp message in one tap.

What it does for you

  • Job card and bill in one: the same record is printed at intake and at delivery, so nothing is typed twice.
  • Proof of what came in: accessories, condition and complaint on a signed customer copy.
  • Open jobs board: every job not yet delivered, with status and promised date, late ones highlighted.
  • Parts and labour with GST: each part at its own rate, labour as a repair service, CGST/SGST or IGST worked out.
  • Estimate guard: a warning when the bill runs more than 10% above the estimate.
  • WhatsApp messages: "received" and "ready for pickup" texts with the job number and amount.

Your data stays with you

Everything you type is kept in this browser on this device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to us. Your businesses, customers, suppliers and items are shared with the other Nivesh Sahayak document tools through my.niveshsahayak.in, so details you saved while making an invoice or a quotation are already here as suggestions.

Keep a backup. If the browser's data is cleared, saved work in it is gone. Open My data (top of the page) and press Save a backup file now and then. One file holds every document, customer and item, and restores them on any other computer or phone.

Questions people ask

Is this job card maker free?

Yes. Job cards, service bills, the open jobs board, printing and sharing are free, with no login and no limit.

Can I use it for a garage?

Yes. Choose Vehicle as the type to record registration number and odometer, and add parts and labour lines.

Does it work for mobile and laptop repair?

Yes. Record the IMEI or serial number, accessories and condition, and the complaint, then bill parts and service.

How is GST shown on the bill?

Each part at its own GST rate, labour as a repair service (SAC 9987, 18%), with CGST/SGST or IGST. See GST on repair bills.

Can I give two copies?

Yes, a customer copy and a workshop copy, each on its own page.

How do I know which jobs are pending?

Press "Open jobs": every job not yet delivered, sorted by promised date, late ones highlighted.

Can I message the customer?

Yes. "WhatsApp the customer" opens a ready message for received or ready-for-pickup jobs. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

Where is my data kept?

In this browser on this device, shared with the other Nivesh Sahayak tools through my.niveshsahayak.in. Nothing is sent to us. Save a backup file regularly.
